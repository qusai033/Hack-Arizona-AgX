# CanopyGuard | Three-minute judge pitch

Read only the quoted text. Click each numbered control at the start of its segment. Allow the remaining seconds in each segment for the visual cue and the handoff. The team’s four slots are 45 seconds each.

## 0:00–0:45 | Teammate 1 | Ground illusion

Click **1 · Ground illusion**. Point at the temperature and wind. Hold the ground-only screen for the handoff.

> You’re in a tractor cab in an almond orchard. The ground station reads sixty-four degrees, with wind from the north at four point eight miles per hour. The ground-only screen gives you a green GO. Would you spray? This is the problem we built CanopyGuard to show. The weather where the applicator sits can differ sharply from the weather above the trees. Our simulator recreates that gap without field hardware. Right now you’re seeing exactly what the five-foot station sees. Let’s look twenty-five feet higher.

## 0:45–1:30 | Teammate 2 | Canopy trap

Click **2 · Canopy trap**. Point at the reason cards, then the canopy view.

> At thirty feet, the air is four and a half degrees warmer. The wind is twelve point four miles per hour from the east, a ninety-five-degree shift from the ground. Warm air above cooler air can limit vertical mixing, and the stronger wind aloft can carry droplets away from the intended area. CanopyGuard changes the decision to STOP and shows the measurements behind it. The alert preview gives the applicator and the PCA the block, the reason, and the audit ID. The same observation still reads GO at ground level.

## 1:30–2:15 | Teammate 3 | Volatility and forecast

Click **3 · Volatility & forecast**. Point at the post-spray notification, then the green forecast interval.

> Risk can change after the tractor leaves. In this replay we finished a high-volatility treatment at three fifteen. At five fifteen, the ground has cooled enough to create a three point eight degree inversion. CanopyGuard raises a post-spray alert using the product profile recorded at treatment. The forecast timeline also helps plan the next pass. The bundled demo shows a screening window from nine in the morning until one thirty. For current planning, the dashboard can pull hourly National Weather Service data for six California locations, including Fresno and Modesto, and model both orchard heights.

## 2:15–3:00 | Teammate 4 | Audit and export

Click **4 · Audit & CSV**. Point to the newest **HIDDEN RISK CAUGHT** entry, then show the download or print report button.

> Here is the record of that decision. The log preserves the observation time, both sensor heights, the computed differences, the pesticide profile, and the exact rules that fired. This filter keeps the moments where the ground station said GO and CanopyGuard said STOP. We can export those records as a CSV for the PCA or print a report from the same selection. The alert and the record share an ID, so the team can trace the warning back to its evidence. The application decision still belongs to the applicator following the product label and on-site checks.
